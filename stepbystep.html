How to Brush and Floss Your Teeth

Brushing Your Teeth

Brushing too hard can damage your gums. Use gentle pressure and let the bristles do the work!

  1. Choose a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste.
  2. Place your toothbrush at a 45-degree angle to your gums.
  3. Gently move the brush back and forth in short (tooth-wide) strokes.
  4. Brush all surfaces of your teeth: outer, inner, and chewing surfaces.
  5. Don't forget to brush your tongue to remove bacteria and freshen your breath.
  6. Brush for at least two minutes, twice a day.

Flossing Your Teeth

Flossing helps remove plaque between your teeth where brushes can’t reach.

  1. Use about 18 inches of floss, winding most of it around your middle fingers.
  2. Hold the floss tightly between your thumbs and forefingers.
  3. Gently slide the floss between your teeth using a sawing motion.
  4. Curve the floss around each tooth in a C shape and slide it under the gumline.
  5. Use clean sections of floss as you move from tooth to tooth.
  6. Floss at least once a day, preferably before bedtime.
