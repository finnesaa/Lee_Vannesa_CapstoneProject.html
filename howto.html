Overview of Brushing and Flossing

Brushing and flossing your teeth every day helps keep your mouth clean and healthy. When you brush, you wash away bits of food and sticky plaque that can cause cavities and bad breath. Flossing reaches the spots your toothbrush can’t get to, like between your teeth. Taking care of your teeth keeps your smile bright, your breath fresh, and helps you stay healthy as you grow!

For detailed instructions on how to brush and floss effectively, please refer to our Step-by-Step Guide.
