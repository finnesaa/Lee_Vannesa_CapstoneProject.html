FAQs

Brushing and flossing help remove plaque and food particles from your teeth, preventing cavities and gum disease.

You should brush your teeth at least twice a day, ideally in the morning and before bedtime.

Flossing should be done at least once a day, preferrably at night, to clean between your teeth where a toothbrush can't reach.

A soft-bristled toothbrush is recommended to effectively clean your teeth without damaging your gums.

Use a fluoride toothpaste to help strengthen your tooth enamel and prevent cavities.

For children who have trouble spitting, use the amount of a grain of rice. This amount of flouride is safe to swallow.
